Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Data we access

  • The authorizing account's own Pinterest profile, boards and Pins.
  • Analytics for the account's own Pins (impressions, saves, outbound clicks).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Pinterest.

2. How we use data

Data is used only to publish and manage the creator's own Pins and to measure their performance. It is not sold, used for advertising profiles, or used to train AI models.

3. Sharing

We do not share data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Pinterest as required by the API.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and statistics are stored on a private server accessible only to the owner and are never published.

5. Other users

The App does not collect personal data about other Pinterest users.

6. Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in Pinterest Settings → Security → Apps. Stored tokens are then deleted; you may request deletion of any stored data via the contact below.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
